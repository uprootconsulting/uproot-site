合作方式How we work

你帶來問題，我們找出真正需要改變的地方Bring us the problem. We’ll figure out what actually needs to change

我們不會一開始就假設答案是一個網站、一套 CRM、一個廣告計劃、一套培訓課程或一套 AI 系統。We don’t begin by assuming the answer is a website, a CRM, an advertising campaign, a training programme or an AI system.

我們先了解真正令事業停滯的原因，再決定哪些部分需要重新設計、哪些需要建立。有時答案是一套系統，有時是定價，有時是整個商業模式。答案由問題決定，不是由我們想賣甚麼決定。We first understand what is actually preventing the business from moving forward. Then we decide what needs to be redesigned and what needs to be built. Sometimes the answer is a system; sometimes it’s pricing; sometimes it’s the business model itself. The problem decides — not what we happen to sell.

如果你已經非常確定答案是甚麼，你可能不需要我們。If you already know the solution, you probably don’t need us.
01

看清問題See the real problem

由一次初步諮詢開始：了解你的事業，找出真正卡住的位置。It starts with an initial consultation: we learn your business and find where it is actually stuck.

02

確認方案Agree the scope

雙方確認項目目標、工作範圍、交付成果及費用。We confirm objectives, scope, deliverables and investment — together, before work begins.

03

重新設計與建立Redesign and build

需要改的重新設計，需要有的動手建立，直至可以面向市場。What needs changing gets redesigned; what’s missing gets built — until it’s ready for the market.

04

交還給你Hand it back

我們的目標是讓事業具備自行運轉的能力，而不是長期依賴我們。The goal is a business that runs on its own — not one that permanently depends on us.
